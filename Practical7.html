Official League Rules & Regulations!

To preserve a safe, fair, and positive competitive environment, all coaches, players, and spectators must follow the Schererville Soccer League handbook rules.

1. Match Duration & Ball Sizes:

  • U10 Divisions: Two 25-minute halves, Ball Size 4.
  • U12 Divisions: Two 30-minute halves, Ball Size 4.
  • U14 & Above: Two 35-minute halves, Ball Size 5.

2. Roster & Substitution Rules:

Substitutions can be executed with the referee's permission during any stoppage in play (e.g., goal kicks, throw-ins for the possessing team, or following an injury).

3. Conduct Policy:

Zero tolerance for foul language, official dissent, or unsportsmanlike behavior. Coaches are completely responsible for the conduct of their team's spectators.
